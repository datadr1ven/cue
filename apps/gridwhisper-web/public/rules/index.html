What we alert on

GridWhisper follows live Formula 1 timing and posts a short stream of high-signal moments — not every sector time or place swap. Alerts depend on session type. Live timing can lag; this project is unofficial.

Practice (FP1–FP3)

You get

  • Session start and finish
  • End-of-session recap (fastest lap, tyre compounds, busiest pit stops)

You don’t

  • Ongoing position changes
  • Pit stops or team radio during the session
  • Small “someone moved up a few places” alerts

Qualifying (and sprint shootout)

You get

  • Start of Q1, Q2, and Q3 (or SQ segments)
  • A limited number of session-best lap times in Q1/Q2 (capped and spaced out)
  • Chequered flag, plus board drama near the elimination cut line
  • Q1/Q2 cut summary when the next segment begins
  • Q3 provisional pole when the chequered flag falls
  • Updated provisional top three if P1–P3 change after the flag (drivers still completing flying laps)

You don’t

  • Every improved sector or purple time
  • Race-style “big place swing” alerts
  • Session-best spam in Q3
  • Sunday grid penalties (qualifying order ≠ starting grid)
  • A fixed “wait N seconds then finalize” timer — updates follow the live timing board

Race (and sprint race)

You get

  • Green flag, chequered flag, and finish (including finishes under safety car when relevant)
  • Lead change into P1
  • Large place swings only (about five places or more, with filters and cooldowns)
  • Pit stops — emphasized for leading cars under safety car
  • Safety car, virtual safety car, and red flag
  • A small number of team-radio clips from the top three
  • Rain starting; notable steward or race-control notes

You don’t

  • Full order updates every few seconds
  • One- or two-place swaps
  • Every radio transmission
  • Routine tyre changes during a red-flag stoppage

Common questions

Why no alert when someone gained two places in Q3?
Place-swing alerts are for the race (and need a large jump). In qualifying, attention goes to provisional pole and top-three updates instead.
Why did the Q3 top three change after the chequered flag?
Drivers already on a flying lap can still set a time after the flag. If that reshuffles P1–P3, GridWhisper posts an updated provisional top three. There is no separate countdown that “locks” the result.
Why does press coverage say a different starting position than we showed?
Qualifying classification and the Sunday starting grid can differ after grid penalties (for example a power-unit component). Live timing during quali reflects on-track order; penalties are not always present in that feed.
Why not every purple sector in Q1?
Session-best alerts are capped and spaced so the feed stays readable.
