What is GridWhisper?

GridWhisper follows live F1 timing and posts the moments that matter — race start, safety cars, retirements, pit stops, lead changes — when they happen.

This page is a live feed of those moments. No account needed to watch.

Want the same alerts on your phone? Tap Get on your phone to open the Telegram bot and press Start. Quiet between moments; /stop anytime.

Live timing can lag and ordering can be imperfect. Unofficial fan project — not affiliated with Formula 1, the FIA, or Formula One Group.

Curious what counts as a “moment”? What we alert on — practice vs quali vs race, in plain language.
