What we alert on

TPlus watches a launch webcast and posts the milestones that matter — not every second of the countdown. Prefer silence between beats. Schedules and streams can lag; this is unofficial.

On a typical flight

You get

  • Liftoff
  • Max-Q, MECO / stage sep, fairing (when on the plan)
  • Boostback / entry / landing burns
  • Booster landing, splash, or catch
  • Payload deploy start / done
  • Hold / scrub / go when marked

You don’t

  • Every T− tick
  • Every graphic or camera cut
  • Guaranteed Max-Q if the timeline is empty
  • Official range or provider status

Why didn’t I get …?

Only liftoff on a rideshare?
Timelines come from Launch Library 2. Some flights publish NET and a webcast with almost no T+ events — we won’t invent Max-Q.
An audio footnote on an alert?
Optional speech spotting can corroborate a milestone. The mission clock and plan stay primary.
Something I saw on the stream that never pinged?
If it isn’t on that mission’s plan (or the desk didn’t mark it), it usually stays quiet on purpose.

Where this is going

Today the desk leans on the on-screen clock plus the published plan. Next is a chain of weak indicators from the same stream — audio and video cues that vote, rather than spam.
