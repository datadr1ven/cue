What is TPlus?

TPlus watches a launch webcast and posts the milestones that matter — liftoff, Max-Q, stage separation, landing — when they happen on the clock.

This page is a live feed of those moments. No account needed to watch.

Want the same alerts on your phone? Tap Get on your phone to open the Telegram bot and press Start. Quiet between milestones; /stop anytime.

Timelines come from Launch Library 2; alerts follow the webcast mission clock. Unofficial fan project — not affiliated with SpaceX or any launch provider.

Curious what counts as a milestone? What we alert on — or how sensing works for the detection roadmap.
